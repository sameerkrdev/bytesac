Bytesac is an on-chain investment platform centered around investment
strategies/baskets.

The product should make investing feel like:

"I am investing in a strategy"

rather than:

"I am manually trading many individual crypto assets."

Core journey:

DISCOVER
→ RESEARCH

→ REBALANCE

→ On-chain execution.

# 5. CORE PROBLEM

Investing across crypto, tokens, chains and emerging on-chain/RWA
opportunities is fragmented.

Users often have to:

- discover opportunities
- research assets
- research managers
- understand allocations
- manage multiple wallets
- interact with multiple chains
- execute multiple transactions
- monitor performance
- maintain allocations
- understand rebalances
- manually manage portfolio changes.

Existing crypto products often expose the underlying technical
complexity instead of hiding unnecessary complexity.

Bytesac should abstract complexity without hiding important information.

# 6. OUR SOLUTION

Bytesac provides:

## Baskets

Curated investment strategies with defined allocations.

## Managers

Verified strategy creators operating through organizations.

## Research

Deep research into baskets, managers, allocations, performance,
strategy and risk information.

## Portfolio

A clear view of the user's actual investments and positions.

## Multi-chain experience

A unified experience across supported chains and assets.

## Investment execution

Users review and authorize investments.

## Rebalancing

rebalance events.
