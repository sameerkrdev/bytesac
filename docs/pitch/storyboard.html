03 · Current solutions and their cons

One matrix, Bytesac row last

Job
Show the gap: nobody combines self-custody, accountable managers, consent and multi-chain RWAs.
Cons
  • smallcase proves the model (10M+ investors, SEBI-registered managers) but is Indian equities only.
  • eToro is custodial; "Popular Investors" are users who meet thresholds.
  • Symmetry gives you a basket token, not the assets; anyone can create one; Solana only.
  • Glider: self-built portfolios, automation on delegated permissions, no accountable manager.
  • Vaults pool custody under a manager or curator.
  • Tokenized-stock venues sell single assets, no portfolio.

07 · Investment flow, drift and rebalancing

User view on the left, engine on the right

User
Drifted position shows "Rebalance to target" or "Keep custom". A new manager version shows the rationale, the diff and a plan; the holder applies or skips. Skipped versions are never replayed.
Engine
Reconcile → thresholds (50 bps / $5) → sells to USDC on Solana → buys scaled to real proceeds → basket cash for leftovers → shortfall repair (Buy back / Sync). Deeper in A3.
Motion
The drift line draws, crosses the band, "Drifted" pops; then the sell → USDC → buy chips light in sequence.
Say
"When prices drift or your manager publishes an update, you see why, what changes and what it costs. One tap to apply, one to skip."
